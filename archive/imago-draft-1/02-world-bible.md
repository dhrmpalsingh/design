# IMAGO — World Bible

## The City: VERTA

A megacity that ran out of ground and kept building up. Around 140 towers, each **400 to 1,000 floors**, rise from a permanent brown inversion layer. No one living has seen the city's street level.

### Altitude is class

| Zone | Floors | Air | Who lives there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Murk** | 0–200 | Toxic, brown, heavy. Masks with double filters. Life expectancy falls with every floor you go down. | Recyclers, filter-farm labourers, the forgotten. The Authority barely patrols here, because the air isn't worth anything. |
| **The Mid** | 200–800 | Breathable for minutes, burns after an hour. Standard breathers. | Most of the population: technicians, clerks, families like Ira's. Most surveilled zone. |
| **The Crown** | 800–1,000 | Clean, under pressurised glass domes. Masks optional inside. Direct sunlight. | Authority officials, resource holders. The only place you'll see a real-looking sky. |

Floor 612, where Ira lives, is squarely in the Mid: close enough to the Crown to see its glow at night, never close enough to touch it.

### The Lattice

Towers are connected by thousands of **skybridges**: glass tubes, open-air catwalks, cargo spans and pedestrian plazas hung between buildings at every 20 to 50 floors. Together they look like a vast spiderweb strung through the smog. Most people never leave their tower cluster. The Lattice is also where the wind is, and the only place in the Mid where you can feel weather.

### Transit

- **Commuter pods.** Driverless flying capsules that ride invisible magnetic lanes between towers. They carry 6 to 20 people and run in dense streams, like schools of fish.
- **Skylifts.** Express vertical shafts that run 200 floors in under a minute.
- **Private flyers.** For the Crown only.

From a distance the pods look like glowing beads on thread; up close they're scratched, cramped, and plastered with Authority messaging.

## The Allocation Authority

The government. It doesn't call itself one. It *allocates*: air, water, calories, floor space, children's growth-rated masks. Its legitimacy rests on one promise: **without us, there isn't enough to go around.**

- **Motto:** *Every Breath Accounted For.*
- **Visual identity:** A white circle (a lung? a coin?) on slate grey. Clean, calm, medical.
- **Language:** Euphemistic. People are "citizens" or "allotments." Plants are "unlicensed biomass." Growing one is "oxygen theft" (you stole the CO₂ it ate) and "water diversion."
- **Power:** It owns the Peda trees, the filter supply, the food printers and the masks. Everyone depends on it to breathe, so no one can afford to fight it openly.

### Enforcement

- **Wardens.** Palm-sized to dog-sized surveillance drones. Matte grey, a single white ring "eye." They hum at the frequency of a breather valve, so you never quite know whether you're hearing a drone or your own mask.
- **Sniffers.** Sensors in every corridor, vent and window frame. They detect chlorophyll fluorescence, humidity spikes, CO₂ dips and heat signatures that don't match human patterns.
- **Compliance Inspectors.** Human officers like Sel Kader. Their job is to rule on the edge cases machines flag.
- **Mask telemetry.** The real surveillance. Every breather reports breath count, breath rate and blood oxygen. A spike in breath rate flags stress, and stress flags guilt.

## Breathing Technology

### The Breather

The standard-issue mask. Clear visor over the eyes and nose, a filter housing at the mouth, a soft seal along the jaw. A small external **breath meter** glows on the cheek:

- **Green** — calm (BR 10–16)
- **Amber** — elevated (BR 17–24)
- **Red** — distress / ration critical

The meter is **outward-facing**. Everyone can see everyone else's fear. It's a social device as much as a medical one.

The **filter cartridge** twists out from the mouth housing. Its empty chamber is just big enough to hold something the size of a butterfly. That detail matters in Act III.

Variants:
- **Child breathers** have cartoon decals and are "growth-rated." Families have to buy upgrades as children grow; skipping one means a too-tight seal.
- **Sleep masks** are softer and quieter, and log your breath all night.
- **Crown masks** are thin, nearly invisible, a fashion statement. Inside the domes, Crown residents take them off and leave them around their necks like jewellery.

### The Ration

Each citizen gets a daily allotment of **breaths** (roughly 14,000 to 20,000, depending on tier). It's displayed as a countdown on home screens and in the mask HUD. Going over is billed. Running a deficit for too long drops you a tier. Children can be docked for crying too much. Nobody says that part out loud.

### Body equipment

Beyond masks, people wear what the air demands: thin exo-braces for weakened lungs, skin-sealant on exposed areas, eye-shields in the Lattice wind, wrist slates for ration tracking. Elderly people like Oren carry small **oxygen packs** on their backs.

## PEDA Units

*Peda* (पेड़) means "tree."

The Authority's synthetic trees, 10 to 60 metres tall, standing in every atrium, school and transit hub. They're the emotional centrepiece of the regime: proof that the Authority gives life.

- **Outside:** Moulded bark, plastic leaves in an idealised green, gently swaying on hidden actuators. Every few seconds they exhale a visible puff of oxygen mist with a soft chime.
- **Inside:** Electrolysis stacks, algae-free chemical oxygen generators, humidity regulators, pumps.
- **The secret:** For weight stability and humidity buffering, each trunk is packed with a core of **real sterile soil**. Ira services them, so she knows. That soil is where her hidden garden's dirt comes from, and where Titli's descendants will grow.
- **The blind spot:** A Peda canopy pumps out so much oxygen and humidity that sniffers treat its signature as background noise. Nothing living could hide in plain sight anywhere else in Verta.

The children's song about them: *Peda, Peda, breathe for me / I'll be good and I'll be free.*

## Synthetic Life

Nothing is born any more; it's issued.

- **Pets** are android-biological: lab-grown tissue over servo skeletons, with a bio-cell that needs replacing every few years (expensively). Tavi's sparrow, **Chirp**, is a cheap model: plastic feathers, a vibrating micro-pump "heart," a song chip with eleven tunes.
- **Food** is nutrient paste from tower printers, in four flavours that all taste grey. Real produce exists only in the Crown, or as contraband.
- **Insects** have been extinct in the wild for over thirty years. Children learn about "pollinators" the way earlier generations learned about dinosaurs.

## The Bio-Archive

A seed bank and specimen museum on floor 440 of Tower 9, closed twenty years ago when the Authority centralised "all biological heritage." Oren worked there as an entomologist. Most of its collection was seized or incinerated. Some of it walked out in coat pockets.

## Titli: the Butterfly

**Origin:** Ira's grandmother's **Blue Mormon** (*Papilio polymnestor*), pinned in a resin block and passed down three generations. Oren extracted viable wing-scale cells and muscle tissue from it and cultured them in a nutrient jar for three years.

**Construction:**
- **Body/thorax:** A micro-machine frame of salvaged Peda sensor parts.
- **Heart:** Chirp's micro-pump. It moves nutrient fluid through the body, and it **vibrates at about 400 Hz**.
- **Wings:** Living cultured tissue over a filament lattice. The scales are **living chromatophores** that shift colour with its state: deep velvet black at rest, electric blue when active, gold when it pollinates.
- **Eyes:** Two salvaged camera beads with a faint white glint, like a Warden's. The machine is visible if you know where to look.

**Why it matters:** Tomato flowers need **buzz pollination**, a vibration that shakes pollen loose. Real butterflies can't do it. Bees could, but there are no bees left. Titli can, because of the sparrow's heart. It isn't a copy of nature. It's something new that nature needed.

**What nobody planned:** The biological tissue keeps growing. In the epilogue it starts spinning chrysalises in the Peda canopy, using the real soil and the oxygen-rich air. The offspring are more organic than their parent, with less wire and more wing. Life is taking the design back.

**Signature:** Titli breathes, in its way. It gives off a tiny pulse of heat, humidity and CO₂ that doesn't match any human pattern. That's what the sniffers catch.
