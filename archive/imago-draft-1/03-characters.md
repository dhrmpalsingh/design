# IMAGO — Characters

## IRA

**34. Peda technician, Grade III. Floor 612.**

Ira keeps the Authority's synthetic trees breathing. Every day she opens their plastic trunks, recalibrates their oxygen output, and closes them again. No one in Verta knows better than she does that the symbol of the state is a machine in a costume.

She is controlled, precise and quiet, and very good at keeping her breath meter green. She learned box breathing as a girl, the way other kids learned prayers: *in four, hold four, out four, hold four.* Calm is her camouflage.

Under the control is grief. Her mother told her stories of a meadow that her own mother had walked through, and Ira has dreamed about it all her life. The hidden garden is her mother's memory made physical. The butterfly is her attempt to finish the story.

- **Want:** To make the tomato plant fruit, so she can give her son one real thing.
- **Need:** To stop doing it alone. She has to learn that the fight isn't hers to carry quietly.
- **Arc:** From hiding life to giving her breath for it.
- **Signature gesture:** Two fingers pressed to the meter on her cheek as she counts her breathing down.
- **Casting note:** Someone who can act entirely with their eyes. The mask covers most of the face for most of the film.

## TAVI

**8. Ira's son.**

Born in the mask. He has never felt wind on his face without a seal. Grass, rain and bees are words in a learning app to him. He is curious, stubborn and funny, and he asks the questions adults have stopped asking.

His closest friend is **Chirp**, a cheap synthetic sparrow that sings eleven songs. When Chirp's cell runs out, Tavi makes the film's first great sacrifice: he gives up his dead friend so something new can fly.

He names the butterfly **Titli**. He is the one who carries hope forward when his mother can't.

- **Want:** For Chirp to fly again.
- **Need:** To believe in a world he has never seen.
- **Arc:** From a child who's told about life to a child who protects it.
- **Signature detail:** His child-breather has a faded cartoon sun decal, half peeled off. Its seal is too tight, because they couldn't afford the growth upgrade.

## OREN MALIK

**74. Former entomologist, Bio-Archive. Floor 612, next door.**

A gentle, funny, shaky old man with an oxygen pack on his back and forty years of contraband in his walls. When the Authority closed the Bio-Archive, Oren smuggled out seeds, cuttings and specimen samples, and he's been keeping them alive in secret ever since. He is the science behind Titli: three years of culturing wing cells in a jar.

He has a tremor in his hands that is getting worse. He knows he's running out of time to see anything he's saved come to life.

> "Plants are half a sentence, Ira. The other half had wings."

- **Function:** Mentor, conscience, and the spark of the collective uprising.
- **Arc:** From a man hoarding the past to a man who spends it all on the future. His act-three diversion, tearing open his wall and calling on his neighbours, is the moment one secret becomes a movement.
- **Final image:** Arrested in the corridor, he pulls his mask aside and breathes the air of his own garden. He is smiling.

## INSPECTOR SEL KADER

**50s. Compliance Inspector, Allocation Authority.**

Not a villain. A tired man doing a job he once believed in. He is calm, courteous and observant, and his own breath meter runs a little high: early-stage lung damage from twenty years of Murk patrols before he was promoted up. He has a daughter in the Crown boarding schools whom he rarely sees.

Sel believes the Authority's central argument: there isn't enough, and someone has to decide. He has seen what happens in the Murk when no one decides. But he also inspects hidden gardens for a living, which means he's seen more living green things than almost anyone in the Mid. Some part of him has been quietly collecting those images.

- **Function:** The face of the system, and its crack.
- **Key choices:**
  1. He notices Ira's humidity readings and lets it go with a warning.
  2. He sees Titli's blue flicker inside her mask and hands her a spare filter.
  3. He scans the Peda canopy, reads "NOMINAL," looks at Tavi, and walks away.
- **Ambiguity:** We never learn whether he's protecting the butterfly or simply choosing not to know. The film shouldn't answer that.
- **Signature line:** *"Your filter's failing, citizen."*

## TITLI

**The butterfly. Semi-biological, semi-robotic.**

Living cultured wing tissue from a 60-year-old Blue Mormon specimen, built over a micro-frame of salvaged Peda sensors, with the vibrating heart of a toy sparrow.

It isn't cute, and it isn't a pet. It's strange: elegant, slightly uncanny, sometimes twitchy like a machine and sometimes impossibly soft like the real thing. Its wing scales are living chromatophores that show its state:

| Colour | State |
|---|---|
| Velvet black | Resting / hiding |
| Electric blue | Awake, curious, flying |
| Gold shimmer | Pollinating (heart buzzing) |
| Pale white flicker | Afraid / low energy |

- **Behaviour:** Moves toward light and toward breath, because CO₂ reads as "flower" to it. That's why it ends up on the window, and why it calms inside Ira's mask.
- **Sound:** A soft, fast purr from Chirp's heart, about 400 Hz, which goes up into a hum when it pollinates. If you listen closely, there's a ghost of Chirp's song chip in the buzz.

## Supporting cast

| Character | Description |
|---|---|
| **NEERA** | Ira's neighbour across the hall, 30s, mother of two. Her basil tray is raided in Act I. In Act III she is the first neighbour to answer Oren's call and open her hidden pots, a second time, knowing the cost. |
| **MIRA** | 6. Neera's daughter. Her breath meter flashes red during the raid, and keeps flashing after the Wardens leave. A small mirror of everything at stake. |
