# IMAGO

*a short film*

**Screenplay — Draft 1**

*Story developed from a dream.*

---

**FADE IN:**

### 1. EXT. MEADOW — GOLDEN HOUR (DREAM)

Tall grass, gold and green, as far as the eye can see. Wind moves through it in long waves. The sky is impossibly blue.

**IRA** (34) stands barefoot in the grass. No mask. Her face is fully visible. This is the only time we will see it like this for a long while.

She closes her eyes and breathes in. Slow, deep, all the way down. We HEAR it: a real human breath, full and unhurried.

A child LAUGHS somewhere out of frame. A woman's voice, old and warm, half-heard:

**GRANDMOTHER (V.O.)**
Hold still. Let it choose you.

Ira opens her hand. A **BLUE MORMON BUTTERFLY**, velvet black with an electric blue band, drifts down and lands on her fingertip. Its wings open and close.

Ira breathes in again. The breath catches and stretches, and turns into a mechanical **HISS**.

**SMASH CUT TO:**

### 2. INT. IRA'S UNIT — FLOOR 612 — PRE-DAWN

Darkness. A HISS-CLICK. A soft amber glow on a sleeping face.

Ira's eyes snap open behind a **SLEEP MASK**: clear visor, soft seal, a tiny outward-facing **BREATH METER** glowing on the cheek. **AMBER. BR 21.**

She presses two fingers to the meter and breathes. In four. Hold four. Out four. Hold four. The meter fades to **GREEN. BR 14.**

A small HUD flickers inside her visor:

> **DAILY ALLOTMENT: 16,400 BREATHS — RESERVE 61%**

She sits up. The unit is tiny: one room, two beds, a kitchen wall. On the other bed, **TAVI** (8) sleeps in a child's breather with a faded cartoon SUN decal, half peeled off. His seal is a little too tight. It leaves a red line on his cheek.

Beside him on the pillow, a small mechanical sparrow, **CHIRP**, sits still, eyes dark.

Ira goes to the window.

### 3. EXT. VERTA — CONTINUOUS (IRA'S POV)

The reveal.

Towers. Hundreds of them, rising out of a sea of brown smog, too tall to have tops: four hundred floors, seven hundred, a thousand. Between them, thousands of **SKYBRIDGES** form a vast glittering web, **THE LATTICE**.

Through the web, streams of tiny lit **TRANSIT PODS** flow like schools of fish.

Far above, the towers end in glowing glass domes: **THE CROWN**. The first real sunlight touches only them.

Far below: brown dark. **THE MURK.**

On a tower face across the void, a hundred-metre screen fades up: a white circle on slate grey.

> **ALLOCATION AUTHORITY**
> **EVERY BREATH ACCOUNTED FOR.**

### 4. INT. IRA'S UNIT — KITCHEN WALL — MORNING

A printer nozzle dispenses grey **NUTRIENT PASTE** into two bowls. Tavi, awake, rubs the red line on his cheek.

He eats through a feeding port in his mask: lift the flap, a fast spoonful, close. Practised.

**TAVI**
Did you go to the yellow place again?

**IRA**
It's green, mostly. The yellow is just the light.

**TAVI**
*(carefully, like a foreign word)*
Grass.

**IRA**
Grass.

**TAVI**
Does grass have a ration?

Ira doesn't answer. Tavi picks up Chirp, presses its chest. Nothing. He presses again. A weak, warped half-note, then silence.

**TAVI** *(CONT'D)*
His cell's going.

**IRA**
I know, baby.

**TAVI**
How many breaths is a new one?

**IRA**
*(gently)*
More than we have.

Tavi holds the sparrow against his chest anyway.

### 5. EXT. THE LATTICE — COMMUTER POD — DAY

Ira, in her day breather and grey **AUTHORITY TECHNICIAN** coveralls, rides a crowded transit pod. Twenty commuters, all masked, all meters glowing **GREEN**. Nobody talks.

Through the glass, the city slides past: skybridges, cargo spans, a maintenance crew in harnesses hanging off a tower face.

A screen in the pod plays a soft animation: a cartoon tree with a smile.

**AUTHORITY ANNOUNCER (V.O.)**
*(warm, maternal)*
A calm citizen is a breathing citizen. Remember, when we panic, we steal from each other.

A man's meter across from Ira ticks **AMBER**. Everyone around him shifts slightly away. He closes his eyes, pressing two fingers to his cheek.

Ira watches him.

### 6. INT. TOWER 9 — CENTRAL ATRIUM — PEDA GROVE — DAY

A cathedral of glass and concrete, two hundred floors tall. In its centre stand the **PEDA TREES**: six enormous synthetic trees, sixty metres high, moulded bark, leaves of perfect plastic green, gently swaying on hidden actuators.

Every few seconds one exhales a soft cloud of white mist with a **CHIME**.

Commuters flow past beneath them. Nobody looks up.

At the base of the largest Peda, Ira kneels and opens a **TRUNK PANEL**. Inside: humming electrolysis stacks, pumps, cables. And at the bottom, packed tight for ballast: dark **SOIL**.

She pushes her gloved fingers into it. Holds them there for a moment.

**DESMOND (O.S.)**
You're doing that thing again.

**DESMOND** (40s), another technician, leans on the next tree with a diagnostic slate.

**IRA**
What thing?

**DESMOND**
Petting the dirt.

**IRA**
Checking moisture.

**DESMOND**
It's sterile ballast. It doesn't have feelings, Ira.

He moves on. Ira glances up. A **WARDEN DRONE** (matte grey, the size of a cat, one white ring for an eye) glides past at head height, humming like a breather valve.

When it's gone, Ira scoops a handful of soil into a sample pouch and slides it into her coveralls.

### 7. INT. FLOOR 612 — CORRIDOR — EVENING

A long, low corridor of identical unit doors. Ira steps out of the skylift and stops.

Three Wardens hover outside a door across from hers. Its frame is lined with flashing sensors. The door is open.

Inside, **NEERA** (30s) stands with her hands up. Her daughter, **MIRA** (6), clings to her leg. Mira's breath meter is flashing **RED**.

An Authority officer in a long grey coat steps out of the unit carrying a small **TRAY OF BASIL**: four sad, leggy, beautiful green plants.

This is **INSPECTOR SEL KADER** (50s). He holds the tray carefully, almost gently.

**SEL**
Unlicensed biomass. Four units.
*(to the Warden)*
Log it as oxygen theft, first offence, class C.

**NEERA**
It was for her. For the smell. She's never smelled anything —

**SEL**
I know.

He means it. It changes nothing. A Warden scans Neera's wrist slate. It **BEEPS**: a ration deduction.

Sel turns and sees Ira. Their eyes meet over the basil.

**SEL** *(CONT'D)*
Evening, technician.

**IRA**
Inspector.

Her meter ticks **AMBER. BR 18.** Sel's gaze drops to it for half a second.

Ira presses two fingers to her cheek. In four. Hold four. **GREEN.**

Sel nods and walks past, down the corridor, carrying the basil. A single leaf falls from the tray. Ira watches it land.

Behind Neera's door, Mira is still crying. Her meter keeps flashing red.

### 8. INT. IRA'S UNIT — NIGHT

Tavi is asleep. Ira kneels beside the **WATER HEATER**, a bulky unit in the corner. She unclips two hidden latches and slides the whole thing sideways on a rail.

Behind it, a narrow cavity in the wall glows soft purple under a scavenged grow-lamp.

**THE HIDDEN GARDEN.**

Moss on a strip of felt. A curling fern. And in a cracked bucket, a **TOMATO PLANT**: thin, determined, covered in tiny yellow flowers. No fruit. Not one.

Ira tips the stolen soil into the bucket and presses it down with her fingertips, like tucking someone in.

A soft **KNOCK** from inside the wall: two taps, a pause, one tap. A metal vent panel at the back of the cavity swings open. Through the service duct comes **OREN MALIK** (74), small and shaky, an **OXYGEN PACK** on his back, a cloth bundle in his arms.

**OREN**
You brought dirt.

**IRA**
Good dirt. They feed it to the trees.

**OREN**
*(fondly)*
Thieves feeding thieves.

He kneels with difficulty and unwraps the bundle on the floor.

1. A **RESIN BLOCK**, old and yellowed. Suspended inside, perfectly preserved: a **BLUE MORMON BUTTERFLY**. The butterfly from the dream.
2. A glass **CULTURE JAR** with a hand-wired lid. Floating in cloudy nutrient fluid: a tiny, delicate pair of **WINGS**, grey, translucent, with faint threads of blue in the veins. Alive. Barely.
3. A tiny **MACHINE THORAX**, made from salvaged Peda sensor parts, no bigger than a fingertip. Six hair-thin legs. An empty socket in its centre.

Ira looks at the resin block. Her grandmother's butterfly.

**IRA**
Three years.

**OREN**
Three years, two months. The wings will hold now. The scales are living. They'll take colour once they have blood.

**IRA**
And the frame?

**OREN**
Frame's fine. Legs are fine.
*(taps the empty socket)*
She needs a heart. Something that beats on its own. Small enough. Steady enough.

**IRA**
I can't get a micro-pump without a requisition. They track every one.

Oren looks at the tomato plant. All those flowers and nothing.

**OREN**
You know what we're doing, don't you? It's not a butterfly.
*(beat)*
Plants are half a sentence, Ira. The other half had wings. We're trying to finish the sentence.

Ira doesn't see Tavi's eyes, open in the dark across the room.

### 9. INT. IRA'S UNIT — LATER

Ira sits alone at the kitchen wall, drawing schematics on a slate. Pump specs, flow rates. All crossed out.

A small hand places something on the table next to her.

**CHIRP.** The dead sparrow.

Tavi stands there in his pyjamas.

**TAVI**
He's got a heart. It's in his chest. It goes *brrrrr* when he sings.

**IRA**
Tavi —

**TAVI**
He can't sing any more. He'd want to fly again.
*(beat)*
He can't fly either. He's a sparrow that can't fly. That's stupid.

Ira looks at him for a long moment. Then she pulls him into her lap and holds him, mask to mask.

**IRA**
*(muffled)*
Okay.

### 10. MONTAGE — THE MAKING

*— Ira opens Chirp's chest with tiny screwdrivers. Tavi watches, gripping the edge of the table. Inside: a micro-pump the size of a lentil. She lifts it out. Tavi looks away, then makes himself look back.*

*— Oren, hands shaking, threads the pump into the thorax socket with tweezers. He drops it. They both freeze. They find it in the floor seam.*

*— A **HUMIDITY ALARM** chirps from the corridor sniffer. Ira slams the water heater shut and throws the window vent wide. Smog curls in. She and Tavi sit very still on the bed, meters green, holding hands, while a Warden hums past the door.*

*— Oren grafts the living wings onto the frame under a magnifier. Fluid moves through the veins. The wings stay grey.*

*— A failed attempt. The wings won't open. They sit in the purple light, silent.*

### 11. INT. IRA'S UNIT — DAY (AUDIT)

A **CHIME** at the door. A **SCREEN** beside it flashes: **ROUTINE AIR AUDIT — COMPLIANCE INSPECTOR S. KADER.**

Panic, fast and silent. Ira snaps the water heater into place. The tomato plant's grow-lamp still glows through a seam. She yanks the power cord.

On the counter: the culture jar with the wings. No time. She opens the drain and...

She stops, holding the jar over the sink. She can't.

She shoves it inside the paste printer's hopper and closes it.

She opens the door. Sel stands there with a handheld **SNIFFER**.

**SEL**
Technician.

He steps in. The sniffer's needle wavers. He walks a slow circle of the tiny room. He stops beside the water heater. The sniffer **TICKS**.

**SEL** *(CONT'D)*
Humidity's two percent over norm.

**IRA**
My son. Long showers. I've told him.

Tavi, on the bed, nods too hard.

Sel looks at the water heater. He puts a hand flat on it. It's warm.

A long beat.

**SEL**
Tell him again.

He marks something on his slate. At the door, he pauses.

**SEL** *(CONT'D)*
My daughter used to do that. Steam the bathroom up. Write her name on the mirror.
*(beat)*
They don't have mirrors that fog in the Crown.

He goes. Ira's meter blazes **AMBER**, then **RED**. She sinks to the floor.

Tavi crawls over and presses two fingers to her cheek, the way she does it.

**TAVI**
In four. Hold four.

### 12. INT. HIDDEN GARDEN — NIGHT

Purple light. The three of them crowd around the bucket. The little machine-butterfly lies on a leaf of the tomato plant, still and grey.

Oren touches a probe to its thorax. Chirp's heart gives a single **TWITCH** —

— a tiny, ragged **BUZZ**.

Silence.

Then another. And another. *Brrrr. Brrrr.* A rhythm.

The **COLOUR COMES IN.**

Electric blue floods the wings from the veins outward, like ink in water, like dawn coming up, until the band glows. A shimmer of living scales.

Tavi stops breathing. His meter actually dims.

The butterfly's legs flex. It stands. The wings open, close, open. Its little camera-bead eyes catch the light with a faint white glint.

It lifts off.

It flies badly. It veers, hits the wall, tumbles, recovers. Then it flies well: a lazy, perfect butterfly drift through the purple air of a cupboard-sized jungle.

It lands on a **TOMATO FLOWER**.

The heart **BUZZES** louder, a high, sweet hum. The wings flare **GOLD**. A fine yellow dust of **POLLEN** shakes from the flower and drifts in the lamplight.

Oren makes a sound halfway between a laugh and a sob.

**OREN**
*(whispering)*
Buzz pollination. Tomatoes need a vibration to release. Butterflies can't do it. Bees used to.
*(to Ira, amazed)*
It's the sparrow. The sparrow's heart does it.

**IRA**
It's better than the real thing.

**OREN**
It *is* the real thing. Just a new one.

Tavi holds up a finger. Slowly. The butterfly considers it, and lands.

**TAVI**
*(barely audible)*
Titli.

**IRA**
What?

**TAVI**
Her name. It's what Nani called them. In the story.

Titli opens and closes her wings on his fingertip, exactly like the dream.

### 13. INT. IRA'S UNIT — HIDDEN GARDEN — DAYS LATER (SERIES OF SHOTS)

*— A tiny green bead where a flower was.*

*— Bigger. Tavi measures it with a ruler every morning.*

*— Titli sleeping on the fern, wings folded, VELVET BLACK.*

*— The bead is now a ball, blushing ORANGE.*

### 14. INT. IRA'S UNIT — NIGHT

A **RED TOMATO** on a plate on the kitchen wall. Small, a little lopsided. Perfect.

Ira, Tavi and Oren sit around it. Ira cuts it into three slices. Juice and seeds spill. Tavi stares at the seeds.

**TAVI**
There's more of them inside.

**OREN**
That's how it works. Every one has more in it.

They lift their feeding flaps. Tavi hesitates, then lifts his whole mask for one second. One breath of unfiltered air.

He eats the slice.

His face changes. He has never tasted anything. He starts to cry and laugh at the same time, mask back down, fogging. His meter flickers amber, and for once nobody cares.

Oren closes his eyes while he chews, as if he's remembering something from sixty years ago.

Ira watches them both. Her meter stays perfectly green.

### 15. INT. IRA'S UNIT — DAY

Empty. Ira is at work, Tavi at school. The water heater is slid back one centimetre too far, leaving a thin crack of purple light.

Titli crawls through the gap.

She flutters across the room toward the brightest thing she can find: the **WINDOW**, where a rare beam of sunlight cuts through a gap in the smog.

She lands on the glass, blue wings open, glowing in the light.

Outside, a **WARDEN** on a routine sweep glides past.

It stops.

Its white ring-eye contracts. A thin scanning line passes over the window.

### 16. INT. ALLOCATION AUTHORITY — COMPLIANCE COMMAND — DAY

A dim room of screens and quiet analysts. A 3D map of the tower cluster turns slowly on a central display, with thousands of tiny green dots: citizens' breath telemetry.

One dot turns **WHITE**. An alert in clean sans-serif:

> **UNCLASSIFIED BIOSIGNATURE — TOWER 6 — FLOOR 612**
> **PROFILE: FAUNA**

A young **COMMAND TECH** frowns, taps, and taps again.

**COMMAND TECH**
Sir? Inspector Kader?

Sel looks up from a coffee he's drinking through a straw port.

**COMMAND TECH** *(CONT'D)*
Sniffer's calling it fauna.

**SEL**
There's no fauna.

**COMMAND TECH**
*(reading)*
Wingbeat signature. Ninety hertz. Something with a heartbeat, sir. Small.

Sel walks to the screen and stares at the white dot on floor 612. He knows which unit it is.

**COMMAND TECH** *(CONT'D)*
Dispatch?

A long beat. Sel's own meter ticks **AMBER**.

**SEL**
...Dispatch.

### 17. INT. TOWER 9 — ATRIUM — PEDA GROVE — SAME

Ira is inside a Peda trunk when her wrist slate **BUZZES**. A message from Oren, a single image: Titli on the window, and a Warden's ring-eye beyond the glass.

Ira drops her tools and runs.

### 18. INT. FLOOR 612 — CORRIDOR — DAY

The skylift doors open. Ira sprints out. At the far end of the corridor, beyond the windows, **A SWARM OF WARDENS**, a dozen of them, is rising up the tower face toward 612.

Tavi is already in the corridor, home from school, his bag over his shoulder.

**IRA**
Inside. Now.

### 19. INT. IRA'S UNIT — CONTINUOUS

Ira cups Titli off the window glass, pale white with fear. Into the empty **CULTURE JAR**, lid on. Titli beats against the glass.

**TAVI**
Mama, the tomato —

**IRA**
Leave it.

**TAVI**
Mama —

She grabs his hand. They dive into the hidden garden cavity, through the vent, into the **SERVICE DUCT**.

### 20. INT. OREN'S UNIT — CONTINUOUS

Oren watches from his window. Wardens are converging on Ira's unit next door. He can hear Ira and Tavi scrambling through the duct in his wall, going down.

He looks at his own wall. He's been hiding it for forty years.

He takes a hammer from his toolbox.

### 21. INT. OREN'S UNIT — CONTINUOUS

**CRACK.** The hammer goes through the false wall. Oren tears the panel away with both hands, his oxygen pack rattling.

Behind it: **A JUNGLE.**

Floor to ceiling, a narrow, impossible garden. Hanging vines, a lemon sapling, chillies, mint, ferns, a tiny orchid, seed jars on every shelf, all under ancient grow-lamps. Forty years of contraband life.

Every sensor in the corridor **SHRIEKS**.

### 22. INT. FLOOR 612 — CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS

The Warden swarm, inches from Ira's door, **SWIVELS** toward Oren's unit as one.

Oren steps into the corridor. He hammers on the next door. And the next.

**OREN**
*(roaring, for the first time in his life)*
Open them! Open them! All of them!

Silence. Doors stay shut.

Then **NEERA's DOOR** opens. She stands there. She looks at Oren, at the Wardens, at her daughter behind her.

She walks back inside, and comes out with a **PAPER CUP**. In it, a single basil seedling, regrown from a cutting they missed.

She sets it on the corridor floor.

The sensors above her door scream.

Another door opens. An old man with a pot of aloe. A teenager with a jar of moss. A couple carrying a window box of spring onions between them. A child with a single bean sprout in a bottle cap.

Doors are opening all down the corridor.

### 23. INT. COMPLIANCE COMMAND — CONTINUOUS

The 3D map. One white dot on 612 becomes five. Ten. Fifty. Floor 612 lights up across its whole length.

Then **FLOOR 611**. Then **613**. Then **598**. Pinpricks of white spreading through the tower like stars coming out.

**SEL**
How many?

**COMMAND TECH**
*(quietly)*
...All of them, sir.

Sel watches the map bloom. His face is unreadable.

### 24. INT. FLOOR 612 — CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS

The Wardens split, re-split, spin. They have too many targets. They're overwhelmed.

Two Wardens seize Oren by his oxygen pack straps. He doesn't resist. As they lead him away, he unclips his mask, pulls it aside, and breathes in the air of his own garden. Mint. Lemon. Soil.

He's smiling.

### 25. EXT. THE LATTICE — OPEN SKYBRIDGE — FLOOR 640 — DAY

A wind-blasted, open-air catwalk strung between two towers, eight hundred metres above the Murk. Ira and Tavi burst out of a maintenance hatch and run. The jar is clutched against Ira's chest. Titli beats inside it, pale and frantic.

Halfway across, a single **WARDEN** rises over the railing ahead of them, and hovers.

Its ring-eye locks onto the jar. A projected **TARGETING LINE** settles on the glass.

They stop. Tavi grabs Ira's coveralls.

The Warden's speaker crackles, polite and maternal:

**WARDEN**
Citizen. Please present the unlicensed fauna for allocation.

Ira looks at the jar. At the drone. At her son.

Then she twists the **FILTER CARTRIDGE** out of her mask.

An **ALARM** sounds inside her visor. Her meter goes **RED** instantly: **UNFILTERED — UNFILTERED — UNFILTERED.**

She opens the jar and tips Titli into her palm. She lifts her palm to the open filter port and lets Titli crawl inside the mask chamber, against her lips, into the warm, wet dark of her breath.

She clicks the empty housing shut.

She breathes in: **RAW CITY AIR.** It burns. Her eyes water. She doesn't stop.

The Warden's targeting line flickers. Searches. Scans her face, inches away.

On its display:

> **SIGNATURE: HUMAN.**

The targeting line winks out. The Warden drifts away.

Ira coughs, a deep, ugly, rattling cough. Tavi holds her up.

**TAVI**
Mama, your filter —

**IRA**
*(hoarse)*
She's breathing what I'm breathing. They can't tell us apart.

Inside her visor, pressed against her chin, Titli's wings slowly turn from white back to **BLUE**.

### 26. INT. TRANSIT POD — MOVING — DAY

Packed with commuters. Ira slumps on a bench seat, coughing into her elbow. Her meter flashes **RED**. Everyone around her shifts away. Tavi sits pressed against her side.

The doors close. Across from them, in a long grey coat, someone sits down.

**SEL.**

He has followed her breath. He's been following it all along.

The pod pulls out into the Lattice. City light strobes across them both.

Sel looks at her. She looks back.

Ira's visor is fogging with each breath, but through the fog, against her chin, there's a tiny flicker of **ELECTRIC BLUE.** Sel sees it.

Silence. The pod hums.

Tavi slides his hand into his mother's. He stares at Sel with an eight-year-old's total, fearless hatred.

Sel's own meter ticks **AMBER**. Then higher.

He reaches into his coat. Ira tenses.

He takes out a sealed **FILTER CARTRIDGE**, standard issue, and leans forward and places it in her hand.

**SEL**
Your filter's failing, citizen.

The pod slows. The doors open. Sel stands, adjusts his coat, and steps off without looking back.

The doors close. Ira stares at the cartridge in her hand.

She doesn't put it in.

### 27. INT. TOWER 9 — CENTRAL ATRIUM — PEDA GROVE — DAY

Rush hour. Hundreds of commuters stream beneath the six great Peda trees. Mist puffs. Chimes.

Ira staggers through the crowd, Tavi pulling her. Her breath is shallow and wet. Her vision swims.

She reaches the **LARGEST PEDA**. With shaking fingers, she slaps her **TECHNICIAN BADGE** on its trunk. A hatch hisses open, revealing a narrow **MAINTENANCE LADDER** spiralling up inside the trunk.

**IRA**
*(to Tavi)*
Wait here. Be calm. Be green.

She climbs.

### 28. INT. PEDA TRUNK — CONTINUOUS

Up through humming machinery, oxygen stacks, cables, mist vents. Past the **SOIL CORE**: a column of real, dark earth bound in mesh. Ira's breathing gets worse with every rung.

### 29. EXT. PEDA CANOPY — CONTINUOUS

Ira pushes through a hatch into the crown of the tree, sixty metres above the atrium floor. All around her: thousands of plastic leaves swaying on hidden actuators. Oxygen vents breathe out mist in great slow pulses. The air here is the cleanest in the Mid.

She unclips the empty filter housing from her mask.

Titli crawls out onto her fingertip, glowing **BLUE**.

Ira takes one long breath of the Peda's oxygen mist, almost the meadow breath. Her eyes close.

**IRA**
*(whispering to Titli)*
Hide where they'd never look. In the thing they're proudest of.

She lifts her hand. Titli lifts off and drifts into the plastic canopy, a speck of living blue among a million fake leaves.

Then she's gone. Invisible.

Ira's legs give way. She sits down hard on a branch strut.

Far below, the hum of **WARDENS** entering the atrium.

### 30. INT. TOWER 9 — CENTRAL ATRIUM — CONTINUOUS

A dozen Wardens sweep in above the crowd. Commuters flatten against walls. Their meters ripple amber.

Two Wardens rise up into the Peda's canopy. A moment later they descend, carrying Ira between them by her coveralls. Her head hangs. Her meter blinks **RED**, slow.

They set her down on the atrium floor. Medics with an oxygen line, restraints.

Tavi, small in the crowd, presses back against a pillar and makes himself very still. Two fingers on his cheek. In four. Hold four. His meter is **GREEN**. He is the calmest person in the room.

Sel enters the atrium. He looks at Ira on the ground. He looks up at the canopy.

He lifts his handheld **SNIFFER** toward the Peda and scans the canopy slowly.

The needle wavers in a flood of oxygen and humidity, then settles.

> **PEDA OUTPUT: NOMINAL**

Sel lowers the sniffer.

He turns, and his gaze finds Tavi in the crowd. Tavi holds the stare. Green. Steady.

Sel looks at the boy for a long, long moment.

Then he turns and follows the Wardens carrying Ira out of the atrium.

As she passes Tavi, Ira lifts her head just enough to see him. She doesn't smile. She just nods once.

Then she's gone.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:** *Six weeks later.*

---

### 31. INT. TOWER 9 — CENTRAL ATRIUM — PEDA GROVE — EVENING

Commuters flow past. Mist puffs. Chimes.

Tavi stands alone beneath the largest Peda, with his school bag over his shoulder. The red line on his cheek is deeper now. His mask is too small for him. He looks up.

Slowly, we **RISE UP THE TRUNK** with his gaze, into the canopy, among the plastic leaves...

A **GREEN SHOOT**. Real. Pushing out from a crack where the soil core meets the bark seam. Small, crooked, alive.

Further up, hanging from the underside of a plastic leaf: a **CHRYSALIS**. Jade green, glowing faintly blue from within, pulsing slow like breathing.

Then another chrysalis. And another. **Five. Ten.**

One of them **SPLITS**.

Something unfolds from it. A **BUTTERFLY**, smaller than Titli. Softer. Its wings are fully organic, velvet and blue, with only a single faint white glint in one eye where the machine still lives.

More life than machine. The next generation. Nobody programmed this.

It opens its wings and drifts down through the canopy.

Down through the mist.

Down to Tavi, who holds up one finger, very slowly.

It lands.

A **BUZZ** on Tavi's wrist slate. A voice message from **DETENTION BLOCK C, FLOOR 312.** He taps it.

**IRA (V.O.)**
*(hoarse, faint, smiling)*
Did it fly?

Tavi looks at the butterfly on his finger. Then up at the canopy, where a dozen more specks of blue are beginning to move among the plastic leaves.

**TAVI**
*(whispering)*
They did.

### 32. INT. IRA'S UNIT — NIGHT

Tavi asleep alone. His mask glows **GREEN**. Beside his pillow, where Chirp used to sit: a paper cup of soil with a tomato seedling in it.

### 33. EXT. MEADOW — GOLDEN HOUR (DREAM)

The meadow. Tall grass in long gold waves.

Tavi stands in it. No mask. The red line on his cheek is gone.

Ira stands beside him, also unmasked, holding his hand. Behind them, at the edge of the grass, an old man with no oxygen pack: Oren, laughing.

The air is **FULL OF BUTTERFLIES.** Hundreds. Blue and gold.

Tavi closes his eyes. He breathes in, slow and deep, all the way down. A real human breath.

This time it doesn't turn into a hiss.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

In the darkness: one long breath out.

**TITLE: IMAGO**
