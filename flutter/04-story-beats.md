# FLUTTER — Story Beats

**Status:** Proposed lock v0.1. This is the exact beat structure, before any dialogue.

**Target runtime:** ~16:30 plus credits, inside the 12–18 minute window. A 12-minute cut is described at the end.

**Dialogue:** None proposed. All words on screen are interface text. See Decisions #1.

---

## Spine at a glance

| Part | Time | Days | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I. The World** | 0:00–3:00 | Day 0 | Everything spectacular. Everything normal. Everything dead. |
| **II. The Room** | 3:00–5:30 | Days 0–8 | Scale becomes intimate. We understand her obsession, and she gives up. |
| **III. Birth** | 5:30–8:30 | Days 9–13 | Something emerges that hasn't been programmed to behave. |
| **IV. Discovery** | 8:30–11:45 | Days 14–18 | It completes a biological cycle, and the system notices. |
| **V. The Hunt** | 11:45–14:45 | Night 18 | The building becomes the antagonist. She refuses. |
| **Ending** | 14:45–16:30 | Day 19 → ~25 | Quiet removal, then elsewhere, then many. |

## PART III — BIRTH *(5:30–8:30)*

| # | Beat | What we see | Sound | Readout |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **14** | **Giving up** *(5:30–6:15)* · Day 9 | She opens the bay **with a waste bag**, to clear it out and end it. The strawberry is alive, barely. One leaf has **chewed edges**, and there's a **faint silvery trail** on the stem. She frowns: rot. Then she sees it under a leaf: **the chrysalis.** Translucent tissue, metallic threads beneath. It looks like mould fused with drone parts, a failure. Her hand hovers with the bag. She **doesn't.** She closes the panel. | Silence except her breath and the hum | *0.006%* |
| **15** | **The pet turns** *(6:15–6:40)* · Night 13 | Dark apartment. The pet, asleep on schedule, **lifts its head off schedule** and turns toward the low wall panel. Its eyes flicker as **the classification loop cycles and freezes.** Ila wakes, because it has never done anything unscheduled before. | A faint organic **crack** from inside the wall | *0.009%* |
| **16** | **Emergence** *(6:40–7:30)* | Lamp on. **The chrysalis splits** along an uneven seam. The creature pulls itself out, wet and crumpled. It hangs. **The veins fill. The wings slowly open**, with the right one still kinked. Moisture beads. The abdomen breathes. | Tearing tissue, a snapped filament, then near silence | — |
| **17** | **First flight** *(7:30–8:30)* | It launches and **crashes** into the bay wall. A **wing sticks** to the wet jar and it tugs free. It **rests** on Ila's sleeve, trembling. Then it **flutters**, dipping right and erratic, out of the bay and through the lamp's light, and **shed scales drift in the beam: the first visible light beam in the film.** **~30 seconds of holding on Ila watching it.** She has never seen anything that wasn't programmed to behave. | **No music.** Wings (the first un-designed sound from something other than her), her breathing, the hum | — |

| **21** | **Countermeasures** *(9:50–10:45)* · Days 15–17 | **Montage. ## Sound plan in one line per Part

- **I:** Designed sound only: chimes, clicks, hum. Pleasant. No silence anywhere.
- **II:** The first un-designed sound is **her breathing.**
- **III:** The first un-designed sound from something else is **wings.** No music.
- **IV:** Panel tones accumulate into a rhythm, an accidental score.
- **V:** **The hum stops.** The quietest act in the film is the most frightening.
- **Ending:** Wings + breath. Silence.

**Music:** Minimal or none. If there is any, it should be one sustained tone under Beats 36–38 and nowhere else (see Decisions).

## 12-minute cut

If a festival slot requires it:

1. Merge Beats 4 and 5 (lift reveal, then straight to the agricultural floor).
2. Cut Beat 7.
3. Compress Beat 13 to two fragments.
4. Cut Beat 20 and move *UNIDENTIFIED ORGANIC MOTION* into Beat 21's montage.
5. Cut Beats 27 and 31.
6. Cut Beat 34.

**Never cut:** 6 (the stolen unit), 10 ("impossible"), 12 (last try), 16–17 (birth), 22 (the bud), 28 (silence), 30 (the pet), 32 (the refusal), and 36–39 (elsewhere, many).
