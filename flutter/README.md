# FLUTTER

*Working title. Short film, ~16 minutes, no spoken dialogue (proposed).*

> In a perfect vertical city where nothing living may reproduce outside containment, a building-systems technician accidentally creates a fragile hybrid butterfly, and with it, proof that autonomous natural life can exist again.

**The civilisation has perfected control. The butterfly is the first thing we see that behaves according to no human-designed system at all.**

## The ideas that hold it together

1. **The system doesn't fear life. It fears reproduction.** A moss patch gets a fine. One grain of pollen triggers containment.
2. **The Authority is rational.** The world was ended by engineered organisms that helped nature and then reproduced without permission. The creature matches that profile.
3. **Healing became reproducing.** The creature exists because a pollination drone's self-repair tissue, cut off from its network, rebuilt itself from butterfly germ cells.
4. **It works the moment he stops controlling it.** The chrysalis forms during the days he has given up.
5. **Every attempt to protect it creates another anomaly.** He's doing exactly what he's paid to catch.
6. **Life thrives in the conditions that are killing him.** When the building cuts his air, the plant and the creature flourish.
7. **His only act is refusal.** Offered amnesty to destroy it, he doesn't.
8. **His hiding place is what spreads it.** The exhaust duct he used to conceal the habitat carries the next generation through the bridges, the city's shared lungs.
