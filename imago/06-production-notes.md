### Practical vs. CG for Titli

- **Practical puppet** (rod or magnet-operated) for: landing on a finger, sitting on the leaf, crawling into the mask, the resin specimen, and the culture-jar wings.
- **CG** for: all flight, colour-state transitions, the awakening, pollination close-ups, and the chrysalis birth.
- **Hybrid:** Shoot every Titli shot with the practical puppet as lighting and eyeline reference, even if it'll be replaced.

## Production approaches by budget

### A. Micro-budget / proof of concept (2–4 min)

Shoot only Scenes **1, 8, 9, 12 and 14**: the dream, the hidden garden, Chirp's sacrifice, the awakening and the tomato. Everything happens in one small room and one meadow. It's an intimate, contained pitch piece that proves the emotional core and the Titli design, and it can be used to raise funds for the full short.

### B. Indie short (full script, ~19 min)

A small stage build for the apartments, location shoots for the atrium and the meadow, and a focused VFX budget weighted toward Titli. City wides are kept to a minimum and carry a lot of the weight with sound design. Achievable with a small, committed VFX team or a single strong generalist studio.

### C. Fully animated

The story works very well in stylised 3D or 2.5D animation, closer to the language of *Arcane* or *Spider-Verse*. That makes the city's scale affordable and lets the colour script push further. The mask performances would rely on eyes and body language, which animation does well.

### D. AI-assisted previs and pitch

Use generative image and video tools to build a **look book**, **mood reels** and an **animatic** from the shot list in `05-visual-and-sound.md`, then use those to pitch for funding or to brief a team. Prompt anchors for the key looks:

- *"Vertical megacity, towers 1000 floors tall rising from brown smog, web of glass skybridges between towers, streams of glowing flying transit pods, Crown domes catching the only sunlight, cinematic, anamorphic, desaturated"*
- *"Woman in clear-visor respirator mask, small glowing amber LED on her cheek, cramped apartment, purple grow-light from a hidden wall cavity, real moss and a tomato plant, intimate, shallow depth of field"*
- *"Semi-biological robotic butterfly, Blue Mormon wing pattern, living iridescent blue wings with visible veins, brushed-metal thorax with tiny screws, camera-bead eyes with white glint, macro photography"*
- *"Enormous synthetic tree in a concrete atrium, perfect plastic leaves, exhaling white mist, masked commuters walking beneath without looking up, cold clinical light"*

## Festival positioning

- **Category:** Sci-fi / fantastic short, environmental cinema.
- **Target festivals:** Sundance (Shorts), Annecy (if animated), Sitges, Fantastic Fest, Palm Springs ShortFest, Tribeca, MAMI Mumbai, BFI London, Clermont-Ferrand.
- **Environmental angle:** Pollinator collapse and air quality make it a good fit for climate-focused programmes and impact screenings.
- **Feature/series potential:** The world supports a longer story: Ira in detention, the spread of hidden gardens across Verta, Titli's descendants reaching the Murk, and Sel's choice catching up with him. Pitch the short as a proof of concept for a feature or a limited series.

## Open questions for development

1. **Sel's ending.** Does he report the Peda anomaly later? (Recommendation: leave it open in the short; it's the hook for a longer version.)
2. **Ira's fate.** The voice message confirms she's alive. Should the short hint at release, or leave her in detention? (Recommendation: leave her there. The cost should be real.)
3. **Language and setting.** The current draft blends South Asian texture (Titli, Peda, Nani, bansuri) into a global megacity. The film could commit fully to a specific setting, such as a future Mumbai or Delhi, which would give it a stronger identity. Worth deciding before the location scout.
4. **Title.** *IMAGO* is the working title. Alternatives: *TITLI*, *Breath Ration*, *The Other Half Had Wings*, *Floor 612*.
