# IMAGO — Visual & Sound Design

## Guiding principle

**Scale outside, intimacy inside.** Verta is shown in vast, cold, symmetrical wides that make people tiny. Ira's world is shown in tight, warm, handheld close-ups. The film keeps cutting between the two. The city is a machine, and the hidden garden is a heartbeat.

## Colour script

| Section | Palette | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Dream (open)** | Saturated gold, grass green, sky blue | Shot on a different lens: soft, wide, slight halation, warm. The only fully saturated image in the first 15 minutes. |
| **Verta: exteriors** | Sepia-brown smog, slate grey, sodium amber | Desaturated. Light comes through haze. The Crown domes are the only clean white. |
| **Verta: Authority spaces** | Clinical white, slate grey, a single white circle | Flat, even, shadowless lighting. Nothing hidden, which is the point. |
| **Peda grove** | Unnatural "screen" green, white mist | The green should feel *wrong*: too even, too clean, like a stock photo. |
| **Hidden garden** | Grow-lamp magenta/purple, real deep greens | The only real greens in the film live here. The purple light makes the space feel like a secret, almost a church. |
| **Titli awakens** | **Electric blue** bleeding into the purple | The first new colour in the film. Let it bloom slowly. |
| **The tomato** | **Red.** | The only true red that isn't an alarm. Stage it so red has only meant danger until this moment. |
| **Act III chase** | Smog brown, alarm red, flashes of blue | Colour becomes conflict: red meters against Titli's blue. |
| **Epilogue** | Peda green with a real green shoot and soft blue glows | Real and fake side by side. |
| **Dream (close)** | Gold, green, blue, now **full of butterflies** | Mirrors the opening shot for shot, with Tavi in Ira's place. |

**Breath-meter colours** (green, amber, red) are a constant visual language. Every shot with people in it should have some meters glowing. The audience should learn to read a crowd's fear at a glance.

## Camera language

- **Verta wides:** Locked-off, symmetrical, long lenses that compress the towers into walls. Slow aerial drifts through the Lattice. Pods move, the camera doesn't.
- **Ira's world:** Handheld, close, shallow focus. Hold on eyes above masks. Use the visor's reflections to layer in the city.
- **Inside the mask:** Several key shots happen *inside Ira's visor*: the HUD, fog spreading with each breath and, in Act III, Titli pressed against her chin. Build a practical mask rig with a camera port for this.
- **Titli's POV:** Sparing (two or three times at most). Compound-eye fragmentation with a faint machine scan line, which is a reminder of what it's made of.
- **The bookends:** Shots 1 and 33 should be composed identically. Same lens, same height, same framing, same wind direction.

## Design: key elements

### The breather (mask)

- Clear polycarbonate visor over the eyes and nose so we can see faces. That's essential for performance.
- A matte filter housing at the mouth with a twist-out cartridge (a practical prop with a functioning empty chamber).
- **The outward breath meter:** a small LED on the left cheek. It has to be practical on set, wired to a remote so it can change colour on cue.
- Visible wear: scuffs, tape repairs, handmade decals. Every mask tells the story of its owner.
- Tavi's mask is visibly too small, with a red pressure line on his cheek (makeup) that deepens across the film.

### The Peda tree

- Should read as **almost** a tree from a distance and **obviously** fake up close: seams in the bark, moulded "knots" that repeat, leaves with injection-mould sprue marks.
- Actuated sway that's too regular, like a metronome.
- The oxygen exhale: a visible puff of mist every 8 seconds with a soft two-note chime (descending fifth). It should become oppressive through repetition.
- A practical trunk panel and the base section of the soil core for close-ups. The full 60 m tree is CG.

### Wardens

- Smooth, matte grey, no visible propellers (quiet ducted fans). A single white **ring** for an eye that contracts like a pupil when it locks on.
- Their hum is pitched to sound like a breather valve.
- Small (cat-sized) to read as everyday and invasive, not militaristic.

### Titli

- **Scale:** Real Blue Mormon wingspan, ~12–14 cm. Big enough to read in a wide shot, small enough to fit in a mask chamber.
- **Wings:** Organic and iridescent, with visible veins carrying faint blue fluid. The scales ripple when it changes colour state.
- **Thorax:** Visibly mechanical when you get close: brushed metal, tiny screws, a faint LED glow through the joints. Don't hide it.
- **Eyes:** Two camera beads with a white glint that deliberately echoes the Warden ring-eye. Titli and the Wardens are cousins.
- **Movement:** A mix of real butterfly flight (erratic, floating) and machine moments (perfect hovers, sudden precise corrections).
- **The next generation (epilogue):** Fewer mechanical parts, softer, more organic; only one eye keeps the white glint.
- **Build:** Fully CG for flight. A detailed practical puppet/prop for hand landings, the mask insert and the resin block.

### The hidden garden

- Built as a practical set: a real cavity behind a sliding appliance, about 80 cm deep.
- **Use real plants.** Real moss, real fern, a real tomato plant. The audience should feel the difference between this green and the Peda green.
- Purple grow-lamp light with practical LED strips. Condensation on the walls.

### The city

- Concept reference: Hong Kong's Kowloon density, Mumbai's vertical sprawl, Shenzhen's skyline, with Brutalist megastructure scale on top.
- Visible human texture everywhere: laundry lines on skybridges, makeshift shrines, hand-painted signs over Authority signage, prayer flags caught in Lattice cables.
- The Murk below should never be fully seen: only depth, haze and faint lights far down.

## Sound design

Sound in this film is **breath**. Every design choice serves that.

### The breath palette

- **Real breath** (dream only): Natural, intimate, close-miked, with room tone of wind and insects. Mix it warm and large.
- **Masked breath:** Every character's breathing passes through a valve. A soft *hss-click* on each inhale and exhale. Constant, layered, like a room full of ventilators. **Never fully silence it in Verta.** When the masked breath does stop, the audience should feel it.
- **Ira's breath:** Her breathing is a score in itself. The audience should start counting *in four, hold four* with her.
- **Raw breath** (Act III): When Ira removes the filter, the valve sound vanishes and we hear her lungs directly: rough, wet, burning. It's shocking because it's *real*.

### Signature sounds

| Element | Sound |
|---|---|
| **Peda exhale** | A soft hiss of mist plus a two-note chime (a descending fifth). Repeated every 8 seconds wherever we are near one. |
| **Wardens** | A hum tuned to the same frequency as the breather valve. The ear can't tell drone from mask. |
| **Breath meter shifts** | A barely audible tick when a meter changes colour. Crowd scenes hold a constant faint ticking. |
| **Chirp's song** | A tinny 8-bit sparrow melody, eleven variations. One of them becomes Titli's leitmotif. |
| **Titli's heart** | A soft fast purr (~400 Hz) built from Chirp's motor sample. When it pollinates, it rises into a sweet hum, with a ghost of Chirp's melody hidden inside it. |
| **Sniffers** | Dry clicks, like a Geiger counter. |
| **Authority announcer** | Warm, maternal, slightly too close to the mic. Comforting and wrong. |

### Music

- **Score concept:** Breath-based. A small string ensemble and solo voice, plus wind instruments where the players' breath is audible (bansuri, shakuhachi, low flute). The music itself should *breathe*.
- **Dream theme:** A simple folk melody on solo bansuri. It sounds like a lullaby a grandmother might have sung. Unresolved in the opening; resolved in the final dream.
- **Verta:** Low drones, sub-bass pulses in time with the Peda chime, no melody.
- **Titli's theme:** Chirp's 8-bit tune, re-orchestrated on harp and celesta, then picked up by the bansuri in the epilogue. The machine song becomes the living song.
- **Act III:** Percussion built from mask valve sounds, drone hums and ragged breathing.
- **Final cut to black:** No music. Just one long human exhale.

## Typography & graphics

- **Authority graphics:** Clean geometric sans-serif (in the Helvetica or Söhne family). Slate grey and white. Lots of empty space. Always calm.
- **HUD (mask display):** Thin monospace, pale green on dark, minimal.
- **Title card:** **IMAGO** in a fine serif with wide letter-spacing. White on black, and it should *breathe*: a very slow opacity pulse on the final card.
