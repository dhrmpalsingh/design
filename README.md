# IMAGO

*A short film developed from a dream.*

> In a choked vertical megacity where every breath is rationed and every living thing is property of the state, a synthetic-tree technician secretly builds a half-living, half-machine butterfly — the first pollinator in a generation — and must hide it from the very surveillance masks that keep her alive.

**They rationed the air. She made something that could make more.**

## The story in one breath

Ira keeps the state's fake oxygen trees running by day and hides a real garden behind her water heater by night. Her tomato plant flowers but never fruits, because nothing is left alive to pollinate it. With her son Tavi, who gives up the heart of his dead robot sparrow, and her neighbour Oren, an old entomologist, she grows a butterfly from her grandmother's preserved specimen. It's half living tissue, half machine. When it's discovered, the fight to keep it safe pulls in a whole floor of secret gardeners and forces Ira to give the creature her own breath. She ends up hiding it inside the regime's proudest monument, where it starts to multiply.
