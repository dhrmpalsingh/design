# Fix 6: Get reviews on product pages

**Problem:** Indexed product pages show no customer reviews. Reviews are the main thing that convinces a first-time buyer of a fragile, handmade product. They also make star ratings appear in Google results.

## Step 1: WooCommerce settings (2 minutes)

**WooCommerce → Settings → Products → General → Reviews:**

- [x] Enable product reviews
- [x] Show "verified owner" label on customer reviews
- [x] Reviews can only be left by "verified owners"
- [x] Enable star rating on reviews
- [x] Star ratings should be required, not optional

WooCommerce adds the rating to each product's structured data automatically, so Google can show stars once a product has reviews.

## Step 2: Ask every customer (the step that actually gets reviews)

Send the request **5–7 days after delivery**, when they've unpacked the piece and put it out.

You can do this by hand on WhatsApp, or automate it with a review-request plugin from the WordPress plugin directory. Search "review reminder WooCommerce" and pick a well-maintained plugin with recent updates.

### WhatsApp message

> Hi [Name], this is [Aditi/Ajayy] from Pipihiri 🏺 Your [product] should have reached you last week. We hope it arrived safely and found a good spot!
>
> If you have a minute, a short review (and a photo, if you can) helps other people find handmade terracotta:
> [direct link to the product page's review tab]
>
> Thank you for supporting handmade!

## Step 3: Use the reviews you already have

- If your Amazon listings have reviews, quote the best two or three on the matching product pages, labelled "Review on Amazon". **Don't import them as site reviews.** Google's rules don't allow star ratings built from reviews collected on other sites.
- Ask past corporate clients for a one-line testimonial with their company name for the Corporate Gifting page.
- Ask class attendees for a Google review (see `03-address-and-google-business-profile.md`).

**Never pay or offer discounts for reviews.** Google, Amazon and Indian consumer rules (BIS IS 19000:2022 on online reviews) all treat that as fake reviews.
