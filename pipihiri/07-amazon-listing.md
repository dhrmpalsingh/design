# Fix 8: Clean up the Amazon listing

**Listing:** https://www.amazon.in/dp/B09HSGKFZZ

**Current title:**
> Terracotta Handmade tealight Diya Holders (Pipihiri) Camel Set of 2 (Brown, Pink and Blue_8inch x 5inch x 8inch)

**Problems:**

- The brand is buried in brackets.
- The underscore breaks Amazon's title rules.
- Colour and size are crammed into the title instead of their own fields.
- The capitalisation is inconsistent.

## New title

> **Pipihiri Handmade Terracotta Camel Tealight Holder, Set of 2, Hand-Painted**

That's 74 characters. Short titles display in full on mobile, where most Amazon shopping happens.

Move these into their own fields in **Seller Central → Inventory → Edit**:

- **Colour:** Brown, Pink and Blue
- **Item dimensions:** 8 × 5 × 8 inches
- **Material:** Terracotta
- **Brand:** Pipihiri
