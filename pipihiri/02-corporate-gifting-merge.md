# Fix 2: Merge the two corporate gifting pages

**Problem:** `/gifting/` and `/corporate-gifting/` both have the title "Corporate Gifting India" and both appear for the same searches. Google has to guess which one to rank, so each ranks lower than one page would. Corporate Diwali orders are placed in October, so this is the most valuable page on the site right now.

## What to do

1. **Keep `/corporate-gifting/`.** It's the clearer URL.
2. Compare the two pages. Copy anything useful from `/gifting/` into `/corporate-gifting/`, such as extra photos, client logos, testimonials or a price table.
3. Add a 301 redirect from `/gifting/` to `/corporate-gifting/` (see `redirects.csv`).
4. Move `/gifting/` to the trash. Do this **after** the redirect is working.
5. Update menus, the footer, Instagram links and any WhatsApp templates that point to `/gifting/`.
6. Apply the SEO fields below.

## SEO fields

- **SEO title:** Terracotta Corporate Gifts India | Custom & Bulk | Pipihiri
- **Meta description:** Handmade terracotta gifts for clients, employees and Diwali. Custom branding, bulk orders, pan-India delivery. Coasters from ₹299, planters from ₹399.

> Check the prices above against your current rates. They come from the version of the page that search engines have indexed.
