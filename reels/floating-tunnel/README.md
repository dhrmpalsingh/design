# What if a tunnel floated underwater? (reel, in progress)

Status: paused. The narration is written (`lines.json`) and voiced (`vo/*.wav`, `vo.js`, about 73 seconds of speech); the 3D scene isn't built yet.

Planned story: a Norwegian fjord about 5 km wide and over 500 m deep; why bridge pillars (taller than a skyscraper), a suspension span (record just over 2 km) or a sub-seabed tunnel (over 500 m down) don't work; a concrete tube floating about 30 m down, below ships and most wave motion; sections floated out by tugboats and joined underwater; tethers to the seabed against buoyancy; angled cables and a curved, arch-like route against currents; ballast tanks for heavy trucks; redundant cables; a drive through the tunnel; and the real-world note that none has been built yet, though Norway has studied them.

Built the same way as `../underwater-house` and `../space-elevator` (Three.js scene in `reel3d.html`, `render3d.js`, `../kit/audio.py`).
