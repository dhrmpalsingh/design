# The Three Doors (reel)

`three-doors.mp4`: 1:09, 720×1280. A generic game show with an original host, contestant, goats and car. You pick door 1, and the host (who knows where the car is and always opens a goat door) opens door 3 and offers a switch. Switching wins 2/3 of the time: your first pick is right 1/3 of the time, and otherwise the car is behind the one door the host leaves closed. A 100-door version makes it obvious. The ending plays 1,000 games with a fixed seed (2024): stay 324, switch 676.

Built with `../kit`: narration in `lines.json`, scenes in `scenes.js`. Rebuild with `../kit/build.sh three-doors`.
