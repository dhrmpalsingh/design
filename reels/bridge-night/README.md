# The Bridge at Night (reel)

`bridge-night.mp4`: 1:16, 720×1280. Four friends must cross a bridge at night: at most two at a time, one torch that must go back each time, crossing times of 1, 2, 5 and 10 minutes, and 17 minutes before the bridge collapses. The obvious plan (the fastest escorts everyone) takes 10+1+5+1+2 = 19 minutes. The answer: 1 & 2 cross (2), 1 returns (3), 5 & 10 cross together (13), 2 returns (15), 1 & 2 cross (17).

Built with `../kit`: narration in `lines.json`, scenes in `scenes.js`. Rebuild with `../kit/build.sh bridge-night`.
